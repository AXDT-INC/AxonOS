Before you launch
An EVM wallet (e.g. MetaMask) · Pay with ETH, USDC, or AXGT for prepaid desktop minutes. AXGT holders get a discount on ETH/USDC, and paying in AXGT is the best rate (Get AXGT) · You'll sign a one-time message (no gas, no transaction) · While connected or explicitly detached, prepaid minutes are consumed at 1× per GPU (wall-clock × profile GPUs), including idle time; after Detach, minutes continue even if this tab closes · Detach returns to home while your desktop and jobs keep running and minutes still count, even after this tab closes. Closing an attached tab requests session end; End session stops billing and removes the desktop. At credit exhaustion, compute billing and viewer access stop while the same running container, jobs, and GPUs are retained for a 2-hour top-up grace; it is automatically ended when that grace expires
